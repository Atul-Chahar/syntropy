# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-27

First release.

- **Nutrition:** Gemini photo scanning for Indian plates with a JSON schema, 170-food Indian table with katori/pc units, meal review with "ate more later", Quick add by sentence (Gemini or offline), custom foods, water tracking, day history.
- **Training:** OpenGym v1.3.8 engine (progression, e1RM, recovery), plan week, live workout logger with rest timer and notifications, exercise logging with progression notes, animated form guide, summary, history, routine builder, library with muscle maps, stats, recovery map, body progress with EMA and private photos.
- **Homeostasis:** energy in vs out dial, Mifflin-St Jeor targets on the Target physique formula, training-day carb adjustment, weekly check-in.
- **AI Coach:** streaming Gemini chat grounded in on-phone summaries, attachments, one-tap actions, history.
- **Android:** Capacitor 8, fingerprint app lock, edge-to-edge dark UI, four home-screen widgets, deep links, signed APK.
- **Web:** landing page and a web demo with sample data.
